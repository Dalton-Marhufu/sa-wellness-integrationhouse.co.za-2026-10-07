## Top 3 Issues
- Dated 2017 BeTheme template still using default theme colours (#2991d6 blue / #f48466 coral), giving a welcoming wellness centre a generic, unbranded look with no real visual hierarchy.
- Homepage is a single thin "Welcome" block of text; none of the six services, the practitioners, the rooms or the gallery are surfaced anywhere above or near the fold.
- No conversion path: no booking CTA, no online form on the homepage, and contact details are buried on a separate page — a visitor has no clear next step.

## Top 3 Improvements
- Rebuilt hero using the practice's own vision ("a varied range of services to meet their wellness needs") over their real photography, with prominent Book and Call CTAs above the fold.
- Full services grid using the centre's exact disciplines and practitioners (General Practitioner, Physiotherapy, Counselling Psychology, Counselling & Life Coaching, Podiatry, Occupational Therapy), each with real room photography.
- Added trust and conversion sections: a values block (authenticity, compassion, dedication), a gallery of the centre's real photos, and a prominent contact section with phone, email, address, hours and a booking form.
