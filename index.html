About Integration House

A space designed to feel accessible to our community.

Integration House is a holistic wellness centre, especially designed to feel accessible to the people it serves. Located in Cape Town's Southern Suburbs, our space is home to a group of dedicated and experienced professionals in various fields — offering a number of services under one roof.

Our vision is simple: to offer our clients a varied range of services that meet their wellness needs. With a strong focus on physical, mental and emotional support, our team shares a common interest in holistic wellbeing.
